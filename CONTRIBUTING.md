These terms keep the project's ownership clear. Contributions may be asked to sign a formal Contributor License
Agreement before they are merged.

## How

- `npm install`, `npm run dev`; `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` must pass.
- Keep changes focused; describe the why in the PR.
